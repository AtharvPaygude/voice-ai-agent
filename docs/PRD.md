# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## Project Title
**Aura Voice AI — Multilingual Voice AI Agent for Sales & Service**

## Tagline
*Speak any language. Connect every heart.*

---

## 1. Product Vision
To build a futuristic, real-time multilingual AI voice workforce that handles business conversations across sales, support, and scheduling with zero human friction, fluid language transitions, and autonomous tool calling.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Traditional IVR and voice chatbots are rigid, robotic, single-language, and fail when callers code-switch between languages (e.g., mixing English, Hindi, and Marathi). Businesses lose qualified leads and frustrate customers with long queues and manual transfers.

---

## 3. Target Users & Personas
- **Inbound/Outbound Callers**: Customers seeking immediate pricing, support, or appointment bookings in their native language or mixed dialect.
- **Sales & Support Teams**: Representatives receiving pre-qualified leads and automated CRM updates.
- **Operations & Supervisors**: Managers monitoring live queue telemetry, sentiment trajectory, and language distribution metrics.

---

## 4. Core Capabilities & Use Cases
- **Automatic Language Switching**: Dynamic detection and adaptation without restarting conversation or losing context (English, Hindi, Marathi, Hinglish + 100 expansion languages).
- **3D AI Orb Visualizer**: 9 interactive operational states (Idle, Listening, Thinking, Speaking, Language Switch, Tool Action, Success, Error, Human Transfer).
- **Business Tool Integration**: Autonomous execution of `book_appointment`, `get_product_price`, `check_order`, `create_lead`, `create_ticket`, and `transfer_to_human`.
- **RAG Knowledge Base**: Verified retrieval from uploaded company product manuals, FAQs, and policies.
- **Enterprise Command Center**: Full telemetry dashboard with real-time sentiment analysis, dialect distribution bars, and call metrics.
